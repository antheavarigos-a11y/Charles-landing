The Problem

Australian investors make six-figure decisions with 1990s data infrastructure.

Property is Australia's most significant asset class. It has never had an intelligence layer worthy of it. The tools available today were built for agents, not investors. Designed to list. Not to analyse.

93%

of investors believe property advisers should be formally qualified. Only 41% actually used one last year.

56%

of Australian property investors are cash flow negative. Most don't know by exactly how much.

70%

of investors own a single property. No dashboard. No consolidated view. One annual tax return.

Sources: PIPA Annual Investor Sentiment Survey 2025 (n=854); RBA Bulletin May 2026, Michielsen (ABS PLIDA-WHAM full population administrative data).

More Australian investors are selling than at any point in the last decade — not because the economics are wrong, but because holding is too hard. Compliance obligations, lease renewals, cash flow uncertainty, no consolidated view. The operational burden compounds quietly until it isn't worth it anymore. Institutional investors deploy teams of analysts and proprietary data systems to manage the same decisions you make alone, with a REA search and a Saturday phone call. That asymmetry is what Charles is built to close.
