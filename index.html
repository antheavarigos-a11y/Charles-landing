The Problem

Australian investors make six-figure decisions with 1990s data infrastructure.

Property is Australia's most significant asset class. It has never had an intelligence layer worthy of it. The tools available today were built for agents, not investors. Designed to list. Not to analyse.

93%

of investors believe property advisers should be formally qualified. Only 41% actually used one last year.

56%

of Australian property investors are cash flow negative. Most don't know by exactly how much.

70%

of investors own a single property. No dashboard. No consolidated view. One annual tax return.

Sources: PIPA Annual Investor Sentiment Survey 2025 (n=854); RBA Bulletin May 2026, Michielsen (ABS PLIDA-WHAM full population administrative data).

More Australian investors are selling than at any point in the last decade. Holding is too hard. Compliance obligations, lease renewals, cash flow uncertainty, no consolidated view. The operational burden compounds quietly until it isn't worth it anymore. Institutional investors deploy teams of analysts and proprietary data systems to manage the same decisions individual investors make with incomplete information, at the end of each quarter. That asymmetry is what Charles is built to close.

Who It's For

Investors who take property seriously, and are tired of running it manually.

Charles is built for the Australian investor who holds property as a serious wealth vehicle, not a side project. These investors are serious. The intelligence infrastructure available to them hasn't kept up with the scale of what they own. The analytical depth they need, institutional investors purchase. Charles makes it available to the individual.

The portfolio builder
Started with one property. Now has four. No consolidated view of any of it.

Compliance dates scattered across email. Lease renewals flagged with calendar reminders that get dismissed. Rent reviews missed because nobody was watching. Charles brings the whole portfolio into one place and watches it every day.

The self-managing investor
Knows their tenants well. Doesn't always know what the legislation requires of them this month.

Tenancy law varies by state and changes regularly. Entry notice periods, rent increase caps, repair obligations. Charles tracks the compliance obligations that apply to each property and surfaces them before they become a problem.

The time-poor professional
High conviction in property. Not enough hours in the week to be properly across it.

Strong income, three to six properties, a clear investment thesis. The day job makes it hard to stay across what's happening in the portfolio. Charles runs the watch so you don't have to. You get a brief when something needs your attention.
